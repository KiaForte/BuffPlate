App Description:
1. AI-Powered Dining Hall Meal Planner
- Integrates with Nutrislice to pull real-time dining hall menus and generate personalized meal recommendations based on students' nutritional goals (protein, carbs, fats, and calories).
- Allows students to enter food preferences or cravings, such as "something spicy" or "comfort food," to receive meal suggestions that match both their macro targets and what they're in the mood for.
2. Student Meal Swipe Marketplace (Potential Feature)
- Allows students to buy and sell unused dining hall swipes at discounted prices (e.g., $6 per swipe compared to the standard $12 entry fee).
- Uses dynamic pricing that adjusts based on real-time supply and demand, creating a student-driven marketplace.
- Introduces a futures-style reservation system where students can lock in today's swipe price for use at a specific date and time in the future, protecting themselves from potential price increases.

Audience:
    Students, mainly freshman, at CU Boulder who want to watch 
    their macronutrients. Athletes would especially be interested. 
    It’s annoying to look at Nutrislice and check the macronutrients
    all the time, so now you can use the app to look at it for you.

Vision Statement:
    For CU Boulder students who need to watch their macronutrient 
    intake. CU MacroPlan is a meal planning app that plans out 
    your meals for you without having to look at Nutrislice. 
    Unlike Nutrislice itself, CU MacroPlan doesn’t require you to 
    look at individual food items for protein and carb intake.
