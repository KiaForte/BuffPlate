App Name:
    BuffPlate (Temp)
App Description:
    Pulls the dining hall menu from Nutrislice and generates a 
    meal based on your macro targets (protein, carbs, fat, 
    calories) and what you're in the mood for (like "something 
    spicy" or "comfort food"). 
    Students (maybe) can also sell unused swipes for about $6 
    instead of the $12 door price, with surge pricing based on 
    demand. Feature: a swipe exchange where the adaptive 
    prices are real market prices, and you can buy futures 
    (lock in a price today for a swipe at a set date and time 
    later).
Audience:
    Students, mainly freshman, at CU Boulder who want to watch 
    their macronutrients. Athletes would especially be interested. 
    It’s annoying to look at Nutrislice and check the macronutrients
    all the time, so now you can use the app to look at it for you.

Vision Statement:
    For CU Boulder students who need to watch their macronutrient 
    intake. CU MacroPlan is a meal planning app that plans out 
    your meals for you without having to look at Nutrislice. 
    Unlike Nutrislice itself, CU MacroPlan doesn’t require you to 
    look at individual food items for protein and carb intake.
