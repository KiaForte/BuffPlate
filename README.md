# BuffPlate
An app that helps students get more out of the dining hall by pulling information from Nutraslice and giving them an easy way to plan full meals, so they don't have to wonder what to eat.

# Menu Creation
Auto-Generated Meals - Creates multiple auto-generated meals for students based on what they are in the mood for; if they are looking for comfort, high-protein, or low-carb meals, it will make portioned meals based on the information the student has provided upon sign-up (weight, age, activity level) to ensure that each meal is curated for that specific student.

Allergy Awareness - When creating an account, students can enter their allergies, and the system automatically avoids foods that conflict with those allergies while warning students about any foods with conflicts to prevent harm. Even if allergies aren't entered, meals will show all allergens they contain.

Meal Creation - Using the Map section or the search, students can not only save the auto-generated meals but create meals of their own that our app would properly portion for them or add parts to generated meals making it their own 

# Map
Dining Hall Map - A top image of the dining hall with the stations at each one labeled; this would also allow students to click on the section named "Asian Cuisine" and then see each separate food that they are serving that day. Also, if a student has already selected a meal, it will highlight the sections they need to visit in the dining hall so they know where to go for each part of their meal.

# Saved Meals/Rating
Saved Meals - Students can save/ favorite that our app created for them, allowing them to get notifications when their favorite meal is in the dining hall 
Meal Ratings - Students will be prompted after leaving the dining hall to rate the meal they had, and then our app would push that meal out to more students the higher the rating, and if a meal got a lot of bad votes or has a low selection rate, it would be suggested to fewer students.
