# BuffPlate
An app that helps students get more out of the dining hall by pulling information from Nutraslice and giving them an easy way to plan full meals, so they don't have to wonder what to eat.

# Menu Creation
Auto-Generated Meals - Creates multiple auto-generated meals for students based on what they are in the mood for; if they are looking for comfort, high-protein, or low-carb meals, it will make portioned meals based on the information the student has provided upon sign-up (weight, age, activity level) to ensure that each meal is curated for that specific student and we are not creating the same meal for a 250lb man and a 100 lb woman
Allergy Awareness - When creating an account, students can enter their allergies, and the system automatically avoids foods that conflict with those allergies while warning students about any foods with conflicts to prevent harm. Even if allergies aren't entered, meals will show all allergens they contain.
